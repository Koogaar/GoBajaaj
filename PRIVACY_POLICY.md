### 1. Horudhac (Introduction)
GoBajaaj ("annaga", "anaga") waxay u heellan tahay ilaalinta xogta shakhsiyeed ee macaamiisheena iyo darawalada isticmaala adeegyadeena. Dukumiintigan Privacy Policy wuxuu sharaxayaa nooca xogta aan aruurino, sababta aan u isticmaalno, iyo sida aan u ilaalino xogtaada marka aad isticmaalayso barnaamijka mobilka ee GoBajaaj.

---

### 2. Xogta Aan Aruurino (Information We Collect)

#### A. Xogta Goobta (Location Data - Precise & Background)
GoBajaaj waa nidaam ku dhisan khariiradda tooska ah ee waddooyinka Muqdisho (Live Traffic Map). Sidaas darteed, barnaamijku wuxuu aruuriyaa xogta goobtaada saxda ah (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` iyo `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION`):
- **Saxmadda Tooska ah & Isgoysyada:** Si aan kuu tusno waddooyinka furan iyo kuwa xiran waqtiga dhabta ah.
- **Badbaadada Qalabka & Raadinta (Phone Guard):** Si darawalka loogu caawiyo inuu helo goobta taleefankiisa haddii uu lumo ama laga xado.

#### B. Xogta Akoonka & Diiwaangelinta (Account Information)
- **Magaca oo buuxa & Lambarka Taleefanka:** Si loo xaqiijiyo aqoonsiga isticmaalaha iyo darawalka.
- **Taargada Gaadiidka (License Plate):** Si loo xaqiijiyo in hal taargo aan laba jeer la diiwaangelin nidaamka.
- **Aqoonsiga Qalabka (Device Hardware ID):** Si looga hortago in hal akoon lagu wadaago qalab kale oo aan la fasaxin (Anti-Account Sharing).

---

### 3. Sida Aan U Isticmaalno Xogtaada (How We Use Your Data)
- Bixinta adeegga saxmadda waddooyinka tooska ah ee Muqdisho.
- Xaqiijinta amniga akoonka iyo ka hortagga wax-is-daba-marinta.
- Bixinta adeegga taageerada macaamiisha iyo maaraynta rukunka (Driver Pass).

### 4. Ilaalinta iyo Wadaagidda Xogta (Data Security & Sharing)
