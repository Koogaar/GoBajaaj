Account Deletion Request

⚠️ Before You DeleteKahor Intaadan Tirtirin

🗑️

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days of your request.

Tirtiridda account-kaaga waa waarta oo aan dib loo celin karin. Dhammaan xogahaaga si joogto ah ayaa laga saari doonaa nidaamkeenna gudihiisa 30 maalmood.

What will be deleted:

Waxa la tirti doonaa:

  • Your profile information (name, phone number, photo)
  • Macluumaadkaaga shakhsiga (magac, lambarka, sawirka)
  • Your order history and delivery records
  • Taariikhda dalabyada iyo diiwaanka gaarsiinta
  • Your saved addresses and preferences
  • Cinwaannada xifdinaada iyo doorashooyinkaada
  • Your payment method records (EVC Plus / eDahab / ZAAD)
  • Diiwaanka habka lacag bixinta (EVC Plus / eDahab / ZAAD)
  • Any merchant store data (if you are a merchant)
  • Xogta dukaankaaga (haddaad ganacsade tahay)

What we retain (legally required):

Waxa aan hayno (shuruuc darteed):

  • Transaction records for financial compliance (up to 7 years, anonymized)
  • Diiwaannada lacag-bixinta oo loogu talagalay caafimaadka maaliyadda (ilaa 7 sano, anonymized)

🔄 Alternative: Delete from the AppIkhtiyaarka kale: App-ka ka tirtir

You can also delete your account directly from the SHIRI app:

Sidoo kale waxaad account-kaaga ka tirtiri kartaa SHIRI app-ka si toos ah:

  1. Open SHIRI app → tap Profile tab
  2. Scroll down → tap "Delete Account"
  3. Enter your phone number to confirm
  4. Tap "Confirm Delete"
  5. Fur SHIRI app → taabo Profile
  6. Hoos u qaaxi → taabo "Tirtir Account-ka"
  7. Geli lambarka taleefankaaga si aad u xaqiijiso
  8. Taabo "Xaqiiji Tirtiridda"
