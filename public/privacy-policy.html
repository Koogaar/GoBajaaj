Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 | Effective: November 1, 2026

1. Introduction

SHIRI Delivery ("SHIRI", "we", "us") operates a multi-service delivery platform in Mogadishu, Somalia. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use the SHIRI mobile application and related services.

By using SHIRI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information:

  • Full name and phone number (used for registration and login)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Account type (Customer, Merchant, or Courier)

Location Information:

  • Precise GPS location (when using delivery or courier features)
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Pickup and drop-off locations for orders

Transaction Information:

  • Order history and delivery records
  • Payment method type (EVC Plus, eDahab, ZAAD, or cash)
  • Transaction amounts and timestamps

Device Information:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • Push notification tokens
  • App usage analytics (anonymized)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To connect customers, merchants, and couriers in real time
  • To send order status notifications via push/SMS
  • To calculate and display delivery routes and estimated times
  • To process payments through EVC Plus, eDahab, and ZAAD
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve our services and app performance
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations

4. Location Data

SHIRI uses your device's GPS location to:

  • Show nearby merchants and estimated delivery times
  • Navigate couriers to pickup and delivery locations
  • Provide live tracking of your order
  • Determine your delivery zone and pricing

Location access is requested only when needed and can be managed through your device settings. Disabling location may limit app functionality.

5. Data Sharing

We share limited information with:

  • Couriers: Your first name, delivery address, and phone number (to complete your order)
  • Merchants: Your order details and first name (to prepare your order)
  • Payment providers: Hormuud (EVC Plus), Somtel (eDahab), Telesom (ZAAD) for payment processing
  • Supabase: Our database provider (data stored securely in the cloud)

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

6. Data Retention

  • Active account data: Retained while your account is active
  • Order history: Retained for 2 years for service improvement
  • Financial transaction records: Retained for 7 years (legal compliance, anonymized after account deletion)
  • After account deletion: Personal data removed within 30 days

7. Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your SHIRI account and all associated personal data at any time. To request account deletion:

  • In-App: Profile → Delete Account
  • Web Form: shiridelivery.com/delete-account
  • Email: support@shiridelivery.com

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Anonymized financial records may be retained for legal compliance.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) for database access control
  • No plaintext password storage (phone OTP authentication)
  • Regular security audits

9. Children's Privacy

SHIRI is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@shiridelivery.com.
