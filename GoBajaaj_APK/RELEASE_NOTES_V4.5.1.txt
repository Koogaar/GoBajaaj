What's New in SHIRI v4.5.1:

🎨 Brand New Red Splash Screen:
• Replaced legacy dark background with the official SHIRI Red brand identity.
• Features the signature SHIRI location-pin logo with smooth pulse and progress animations.

👤 Passenger Profile Role Isolation:
• Cleaned customer profile screen: Merchant Management, Bajaaj Driver Pass, and Courier Delivery cards are hidden for regular customers.
• Each user role (Customer, Merchant, Courier, Bajaaj Driver) strictly sees only their relevant tools and dashboards.

🎙️ Real Native Voice Recording for Orders:
• Upgraded the Voice Order Assistant from mock state to real native Android MediaRecorder.
• Supports microphone runtime permission requests, live recording counter, and instant audio playback before checkout.
• Customers can easily record voice descriptions of their orders or house landmark directions.

📍 Digital House & Landmark Address:
• Integrated digital address code (e.g. SHR-MG-XXXXX) for precise deliveries across Mogadishu.
