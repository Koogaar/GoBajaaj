WIXII CUSUB IYO WAXQABADKA V3.9.0:
==============================================

1. NIDAAMKA GURI NUMBER-KA DHIGITAL-KA AH (DIGITAL HOUSE NUMBER):
   - Macmiilka/Rakaabku wuxuu si fudud u samaysan karaa cinwaan gaar ah (tusaale: MG-HODAN-125).
   - Cinwaanka waxaa ku jira Degmada, nooca goobta (Gurigeyga, Shaqada, Baqaalka, Kale), iyo GPS-ka saxda ah.
   - Macmiilku wuxuu si degdeg ah hal taabasho ugu dooran karaa cinwaannadiisa kaydsan.

2. TILMAANTA SOOMAALIDA (SOMALI DIRECTIONS & LANDMARKS):
   - Qeyb gaar ah oo loogu talagalay tilmaanta caadiga ah ee Soomaalida (tusaale: "Taleex, Masaajidka horteeda, albaabka casaanka ah").
   - Tilmaantani waxay toos ugu tagtaa darawalka iyadoo la socota GPS-ka goobta.

3. RADAR-KA 1 KM EE BAJAAJYADA KU DHOW (1 KM NEARBY DRIVERS RADAR):
   - Macmiilka waxaa loo muujinayaa tirada bajaajyada bannaan (free/online) ee 1 km u jira goobtiisa.
   - Waxay macmiilka siinaysaa kalsooni buuxda in bajaaj dhow laga heli karo ka hor inta uusan dalban.

4. BUUGGA DHAQAAHAHA & KHARASHAADKA DARAWALKA (DRIVER FINANCIAL LEDGER):
   - Darawalku wuxuu si buuxda ula socon karaa dakhligiisa iyo kharashaadkiisa (Maalinle, Isbuucle, Bile, Sanadle).
   - Qeybaha kharashka: Shidaal, Dayactir, Canshuur/Baas, Cunto, iyo Kuwo kale.
   - Safar kasta oo GoBajaaj ah oo darawalku dhameeyo waxaa si toos ah loogu qoraa buugga xisaabta dakhli ahaan.

5. ISKU-XIRKA TOOSKA AH EE GOOGLE MAPS-KA TALEEFANKA (ONE-TAP GOOGLE MAPS NAVIGATION):
   - Darawalku markuu aqbalo safarka, wuxuu hal taabasho ku furi karaa app-ka Google Maps-ka taleefankiisa si uu toos ugu tago goobta rakaabka ama meesha loo socdo.

6. SOO CELINTA DOORKA RAKAABKA (PASSENGER ROLE RESTORATION):
   - Doorka Macmiilka/Rakaabka dib ayaa loo soo celiyay si ay dadweynuhu si toos ah ugu dalban karaan bajaajta iyaga oo aan u baahnayn Driver Pass.
