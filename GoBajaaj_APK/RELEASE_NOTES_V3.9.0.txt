WIXII CUSUB IYO WAXQABADKA V3.9.0:
==============================================

1. HAGAASINTA UI-GA DALBASHADA & QORAALLADA DASHBOARD-KA:
   - Kaarka "Dalbo Bajaaj Hadda" ee shaashadda hore waxaa loo habeeyay si ballaaran oo casri ah oo uusan qoraalku isku buuqin (Badges: 🏠 Guri Number, 📍 Tilmaan Toos ah, 🤝 Gorgortan).
   - Qoraalkii "Gorgortan Xor ah" waxaa si toos ah loogu soo koobay "Gorgortan" oo kaliya.

2. KALA REEBIDDA DOORKA DARAWALKA IYO MACMIILKA (NO ROAD TRAFFIC FOR PASSENGERS):
   - Qeybta macmiilka/rakaabka waxaa laga saaray dhammaan xogaha waddooyinka, shilalka, isgoysyada, iyo khariiradda saxmadda maadaama ay khuseeyaan darawalka kaliya.
   - Bar-hageenta hoose (Bottom Navigation) waxay macmiilka u muujinaysaa: [Guudmar, Dalbo, Akoonka], halka darawalka ay u muujinayso: [Guudmar, Khariiradda, Akoonka].

3. DARAWALKU WUXUU SI TOOS AH U ARKI KARAA XOGTA MACMIILKA:
   - Shaashadda dalabka cusub (Incoming Ride Alert) iyo shaashadda socodka safarka (Trip In Progress) labaduba waxay darawalka u muujinayaan:
     * Magaca buuxa ee macmiilka (Passenger Name).
     * Number-ka taleefanka macmiilka oo leh badhanka degdegga ah ee wicitaanka (Direct Call Button).
     * Halka uu macmiilku joogo (Pickup Location, Guri Number, & Tilmaanta Soomaaliga ah).

4. GELINTA SAWIRKA PROFILE-KA (AVATAR PHOTO UPLOAD):
   - Darawalka iyo macmiilkuba waxay hadda shaashadda Profile-ka ka gelin karaan sawir (Gallery Photo Picker) kaas oo si toos ah ugu muuqanaya akoonka iyo shaashadda guudmar-ka (Home Header).

5. NIDAAMKA GURI NUMBER-KA DHIGITAL-KA AH (DIGITAL HOUSE NUMBER):
   - Macmiilka/Rakaabku wuxuu si fudud u samaysan karaa cinwaan gaar ah (tusaale: MG-HODAN-125).
   - Cinwaanka waxaa ku jira Degmada, nooca goobta (Gurigeyga, Shaqada, Baqaalka, Kale), iyo GPS-ka saxda ah.

6. TILMAANTA SOOMAALIDA (SOMALI DIRECTIONS & LANDMARKS):
   - Qeyb gaar ah oo loogu talagalay tilmaanta caadiga ah ee Soomaalida (tusaale: "Taleex, Masaajidka horteeda, albaabka casaanka ah").
   - Tilmaantani waxay toos ugu tagtaa darawalka iyadoo la socota GPS-ka goobta.

7. RADAR-KA 1 KM EE BAJAAJYADA KU DHOW (1 KM NEARBY DRIVERS RADAR):
   - Macmiilka waxaa loo muujinayaa tirada bajaajyada bannaan (free/online) ee 1 km u jira goobtiisa.

8. BUUGGA DHAQAAHAHA & KHARASHAADKA DARAWALKA (DRIVER FINANCIAL LEDGER):
   - Darawalku wuxuu si buuxda ula socon karaa dakhligiisa iyo kharashaadkiisa (Maalinle, Isbuucle, Bile, Sanadle).

9. SOO CELINTA DOORKA RAKAABKA (PASSENGER ROLE RESTORATION):
   - Doorka Macmiilka/Rakaabka dib ayaa loo soo celiyay si ay dadweynuhu si toos ah ugu dalban karaan bajaajta iyaga oo aan u baahnayn Driver Pass.
   - Is-diiwaangelinta rakaabka waxaa laga reebay Koodhka Suuq-geynta (Marketing Code), kaasoo u gaar ah diiwaangelinta darawallada.
