SHIRI is Mogadishu's all-in-one delivery and courier platform connecting customers, merchants, and drivers in real time.

🛵 FOR CUSTOMERS:
• Order food from local restaurants and have it delivered to your door
• Shop groceries, medicine, and everyday items from nearby stores
• Book a courier for fast parcel and document delivery across Mogadishu
• Track your order live on the map — from acceptance to doorstep
• Pay via EVC Plus, eDahab, ZAAD, or cash on delivery
• Multilingual interface: Somali & English

🏪 FOR MERCHANTS & BUSINESSES:
• Manage your store, products, and incoming orders from one dashboard
• Real-time kitchen alarm when new orders arrive
• Accept or reject orders with one tap
• View daily earnings and order history
• Easy product catalog management with stock control
• Request a courier to pick up and deliver your orders

🚗 FOR COURIERS & DRIVERS:
• Receive new delivery requests instantly
• Navigate to pickup and drop-off locations
• Track today's earnings and completed trips
• Manage your availability (Online/Offline)
• Communicate with customers and merchants directly

📍 SERVICE AREAS:
• Hodan, Waberi, Hamarweyne, Bondhere, Shangani, Karaan, Yaqshid (Mogadishu)
• Expanding to more districts soon

SHIRI connects Mogadishu's growing economy with modern delivery technology — fast, reliable, and built for Somalia.

Download SHIRI and experience seamless delivery today.
