What's New in SHIRI v4.0.5:

🔐 Production Role Authentication Flow:
• Dedicated login & registration routing based on selected user role:
  - Customer / Macmiilka (Super App: Food, Market, Parcels, Rides)
  - Merchant / Business Owner (Store Management, Orders, Kitchen Alarm)
  - Courier / Mooto Driver (Express Package & Food Delivery)
  - Driver / Wade Bajaaj (Passenger Rides & 24h Driver Pass)
• Seamless session restoration directly into the user's role-specific dashboard
• No demo/test elements — 100% production-ready native Android performance

🛵 Courier Delivery Dashboard:
• Real-time incoming delivery orders
• Turn-by-turn navigation with Google Maps
• Handover verification with customer 4-digit PIN

🏪 Business & Merchant Storefront:
• Store Open/Close status toggle
• Real-time order pipeline (Pending, Preparing, Ready, Delivered)
• Payment verification and WhatsApp customer receipt confirmation
• Clean product and catalog stock management

📦 Customer Live Tracking:
• Shiri Digital Address (e.g. SHR-MG-48291)
• 1-step checkout with voice landmark notes & telecom USSD shortcuts
• Clear 4-digit Handover PIN displayed to customer to read aloud to courier
