ENGLISH (Copy & Paste into Google Play Console):
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
What's New in SHIRI v4.0.4:

🔐 Role-Based Authentication:
• Each app (Customer, Merchant, Courier) now has its own dedicated login screen
• Users stay in their selected role — no cross-role switching
• Secure logout available from every app screen

📦 Delivery Handover PIN Fix:
• Customer now clearly sees their 4-digit handover PIN during live tracking
• Customer reads PIN aloud to courier upon delivery — simple and secure
• Courier enters the PIN they hear from customer to confirm delivery

🛵 Courier App Improvements:
• Navigation tab now shows live map with GPS tracking
• Orders (Dalabyada) tab displays real available orders
• Clean dashboard without demo/test buttons

🏪 Business App Improvements:
• Products tab shows only YOUR store's products
• Add Product button appears only in Products section
• Orders tab shows only your store's orders
• Finance tab shows real earnings from completed orders

⚡ Performance & Reliability:
• Removed all demo/test elements — fully production ready
• Improved navigation speed across all screens
• Zero mock data — all real platform data
