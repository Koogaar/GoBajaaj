OVERVIEW:
This release represents a complete visual and functional redesign of the SHIRI
Android application, upgrading it into a high-performance, multi-category local
delivery marketplace inspired by DoorDash and Uber Eats, matching the master
design specification with the signature Emerald Green branding (#00A859).

THREE DEDICATED EXPERIENCES (ALL-IN-ONE APP):

1. CUSTOMER MARKETPLACE (5 DESTINATIONS):
   • Bottom Navigation: Home, Search, Orders, Saved, Profile.
   • Master Header: Mogadishu delivery location picker with district dropdown,
     quick "Change" pill, live notifications bell badge, and central SHIRI logo.
   • Search Bar: "Maxaad maanta rabtaa?" placeholder with voice & text search.
   • 18 Marketplace Categories:
     1. Restaurants & Food (Cunto & Maqaayado)
     2. Grocery & Supermarkets
     3. Pharmacy & Healthcare (Daawo & Caafimaad)
     4. Clothing & Fashion (Dhar & Fashion)
     5. Electronics & Gadgets
     6. Beauty & Cosmetics
     7. Perfumes & Fragrances
     8. Bakery & Desserts
     9. Home & Household
     10. Books & Stationery
     11. Baby Products
     12. Furniture & Living
     13. Flowers & Gifts
     14. Water & Beverages
     15. Sports & Fitness
     16. Auto Parts & Garage
     17. Pet Supplies
     18. Other Businesses & Services
   • Special Offer Banner: 20% OFF Green gradient banner with "Order Now ->".
   • Popular Near You: Horizontal cards for Pizza Palace, Fresh Market, Health Plus,
     Burger House, Somali Food, and KFC Somalia.
   • Search Screen (Screen 2): Filter chips (Top Rated, Under 20 min, Free Delivery),
     recent search history, category shortcuts, top businesses, and instant filter.
   • Category Screen (Screen 3): Hero food banner ("Delicious food, delivered fast"),
     subcategory pills (All, Pizza, Burger, African, Seafood), store list with (+) buttons.
   • Store Detail Screen (Screen 4): Pizza Palace cover, rating ⭐ 4.7 (1.2k), delivery
     estimate, tabs (Menu | Reviews | Info), popular items, floating Cart Bar.
   • Modern Cart (Screen 5): Store info (Pizza Palace • 25 min), item list with
     [-] [qty] [+] adjusters, Subtotal, Delivery Fee, Service Fee, Total, and big
     green "Checkout ->" button.
   • Saved Screen: Bookmarked stores and favorite products.
   • Customer Profile: Clean user info, language toggle (Somali/English), district
     picker, and direct "Switch to Merchant Mode" tile.

2. BUSINESS / MERCHANT EXPERIENCE:
   • 5 Merchant Tabs: Dashboard, Products, Orders, Store, Profile.
   • Merchant Dashboard: Real store status (Open/Online), 4 stat cards
     (Today's Orders 24, Revenue $420, Pending 6, Completed 18), and custom Bézier
     gradient Sales Overview Chart.
   • Products Management: Product list with images, pricing, In Stock / Out of Stock
     status toggles, and edit dialogs.
   • Orders Management: Tabs for New (6), Preparing, Ready, with Accept (green)
     and Reject (light red) action buttons.
   • Role Switcher: Seamless switching between Customer and Merchant modes without
     creating duplicate accounts.

3. DELIVERY APP — DELIVER WITH SHIRI:
   • Screen 1 (New Delivery): Interactive vector route map, pickup marker (Pizza Palace),
     dropoff marker (Via Somalia), route metrics, and big green "Accept Order" button.
   • Screen 2 (On the Way): Route map with animated scooter marker, "Arriving in 8 min"
     floating badge, customer contact card (Ahmed Hassan ⭐ 4.9), phone/chat triggers,
     and big green "Contact" button.
   • Screen 3 (Earnings): "$85.50 Today" earnings card, 8 Deliveries, 5.2h Online,
     weekly earnings bar chart (Mon-Sun), and "View Details" button.
