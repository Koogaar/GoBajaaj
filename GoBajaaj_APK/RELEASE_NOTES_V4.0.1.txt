What's New in SHIRI v4.0.1:

🍽️ Dedicated Merchant Storefront View:
• When a customer selects any restaurant or business (e.g. Pizza House Mogadishu), only that merchant's specific catalog and menu are displayed.
• High-resolution banner image, store rating ⭐, delivery ETA, and delivery fee breakdown.
• Quick "+ Add to Cart" action buttons and persistent "View Cart" sticky bottom bar.

🛵 Integrated Courier Delivery System:
• Available Orders dispatch card with real-time audio and visual alerts.
• Today's Earnings dashboard showing completed trips and Cash on Delivery totals.
• Direct WhatsApp/Call driver contact and 4-digit Handover PIN verification.

⚡ Performance & Reliability:
• Faster app rendering and smooth screen navigation transitions.
• Enhanced shopping cart memory and checkout workflow.
